# Interview Presentation Prompt

As part of this interview, we ask that you prepare a presentation for this virtual interview. 

## Guidelines
The goal of this presentation is to explain the value of a technical tool or a technical approach/method to a technology (it can be anything, as long as it is a technical topic)
The topic should be something that you're confident to present and answer questions about
You should assume that Brian does not know anything about the topic you are presenting


## Constraints
- Your presentation should not exceed 15 minutes
- You can come with any kind of materials you feel appropriate for your presentation (live screen-sharing demo, slides, etc.), as long as you can make them work over a Google Meet call
